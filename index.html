The Founder · 0,0 Origin

Ayushmaan Bellum

Ayushmaan Bellum is the founder and lead of Cognitaur Innovation Lab. He is a young engineer, maker, teacher and entrepreneur working across design, manufacturing, code and robotics. He builds fluently in CAD, machines his own parts on CNC, prototypes with 3D printing, writes the software that drives his robots, and researches what comes next.

He's also a teacher, a competitor, a published author and an inventor. Ayushmaan Bellum captains VEX Robotics Team 29712C "Cognitaur," a World Championship qualifier, has taught Python to over 400 young learners, programs robotics in C++, co-authored a peer-reviewed robotics study in the National High School Journal of Science, and holds three patent-pending inventions: Dermalytix, Robopyll and QRHealthX. A student at Laurel Springs School (Class of 2028) based in Folsom, California, his honors include Conrad Innovator at the Conrad Global Challenge (2026) for Dermalytix, 1st place at the Code for Impact Hackathon for SunShiftAI, 3rd place in Applied Engineering at the California Regional STEM Competition (2026), the Think Award at the California State Championship, 3rd place worldwide in the RECF CAD Design Challenge, the Presidential Volunteer Service Award (Gold), congressional recognition for student STEM innovation, and a feature on CBS Good Day Sacramento.

Beyond building, he leads. Cognitaur is his platform to run hands-on camps, mentor peers, open internships, and partner with professionals and industry, turning individual skill into a community and a venture.
